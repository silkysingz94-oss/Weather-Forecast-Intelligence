About the platform

A browser-based weather intelligence prototype for The Gambia. It combines mapped settlements, GPS coordinates, current weather observations and forecast information.

This is not a locally trained ECMWF Anemoi model or an official national meteorological forecasting service.

Methodology and sources

Current conditions and forecast variables are retrieved from the Open-Meteo forecast API. Settlement locations are queried from OpenStreetMap through Overpass.

Forecasts are model-derived guidance and may differ from official observations or warnings.
